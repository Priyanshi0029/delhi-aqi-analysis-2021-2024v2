How polluted, how often

Every day classified against India's CPCB AQI bands. Only 1 in 28 days qualified as "Good" — nearly half fell in Poor, Very Poor, or Severe territory.

PM10 and PM2.5 track AQI closest — Ozone is the only pollutant that moves the opposite way, easing as the others spike.
